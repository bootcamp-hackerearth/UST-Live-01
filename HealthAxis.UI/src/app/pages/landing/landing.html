About Us

One connected healthcare system for patients, doctors, and administrators.

HealthAxis is an online healthcare appointment platform that connects patients, doctors, and hospital administrators through one secure digital system. Patients can find active specialists, compare consultation details, and book appointments without visiting the hospital reception desk.

The platform supports real-time doctor availability, validated appointment dates and time slots, appointment status tracking, digital health records, and secure role-based access. Doctors can manage consultations and create medical records, while administrators can monitor hospital operations and reports.

Online Appointment Booking Find a specialist and schedule a consultation from anywhere.
Live Doctor Availability View only active doctors and valid appointment time slots.
Appointment Tracking Follow pending, confirmed, completed, and cancelled visits.
Digital Health Records Access diagnosis, prescriptions, and visit history securely.
