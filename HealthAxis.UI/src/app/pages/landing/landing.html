About HealthAxis

One connected healthcare system for patients, doctors, and admins.

HealthAxis is designed to make appointment booking, doctor workflow, patient care, and admin reporting simple and secure.

Patients can register, book appointments, track visit status, and view health records. Doctors can manage appointments and create medical records. Admins can manage users, doctors, patients, and reports from one dashboard.
